🌿 Life Oasis Wellness

Welcome to Life Oasis Wellness — a simple, friendly wellness web app designed to encourage healthier daily habits and support overall wellbeing.

✨ Features
🧘 Mindfulness and wellness activities
💧 Hydration tracking
😌 Mood and wellbeing tracking
💤 Sleep and rest awareness
🏃 Healthy lifestyle goals
📖 Personal wellness journaling
📊 Progress tracking
📱 Responsive design for phones, tablets, and computers
🎯 Purpose

Life Oasis Wellness was created to provide users with a simple digital space where they can focus on their wellbeing, develop positive habits, and monitor their daily progress.
